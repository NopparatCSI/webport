## Why this stack

This starter combines:

- **Next.js 14 + TypeScript** for typed app-router development.
- **TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui** for fast, consistent UI.
- **Framer Motion** for expressive interactions.
- **MDX** for content-driven pages.

### Add your own posts

Create files under `content/posts/*.mdx` with frontmatter:

```md
---
title: "Post title"
description: "Short summary"
date: "2026-05-06"
---
```

Then open `/blog` to see them.
