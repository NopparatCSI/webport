You can animate sections in client components while keeping your long-form content in MDX.

### Pattern used in this project

1. Build animated shells with `"use client"` components.
2. Keep post content in server-rendered MDX files.
3. Bind both through simple route params.

This gives you polished interactions without sacrificing content workflow.
